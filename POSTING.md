# Posting playbook — 90 SECONDS

Per-film copy lives in `films/<slug>/post.md`. This file is how to post it.

## The split

| Platform | File | Leads with | Where the "made with Claude" story goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | `<slug>-9x16.mp4` | the subject + one hook line | pinned comment only |
| Instagram Reels | `<slug>-9x16.mp4` | the subject + "sound on" | last line of the caption |
| YouTube Shorts | `<slug>-9x16.mp4` | the subject in the title | description + pinned comment |
| X | `<slug>-16x9.mp4` | the subject in post 1 | **the thread**: the maker story is the X hook |

Sports fans find the film through TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Tech people find the method through X.

## Every upload

1. **Upload the original MP4 from `out/`.** Never re-upload a file downloaded from another platform:
   Instagram and YouTube down-rank anything carrying another app's watermark.
2. **Turn on the AI label** where the platform offers one: TikTok "AI-generated content", Instagram "AI info",
   YouTube "altered or synthetic content" (only required for realistic content, but it costs nothing).
3. **Cover frame:** pick the strongest still, the one listed in the film's `post.md`.
4. **Hashtags:** 3–5 per post: the subject, the league, the sport, one series tag. More reads as spam.
5. **Pin the comment** from `post.md`. It is the question that gets people replying.
6. **Reply to comments in the first hour.** That is when each platform decides whether to push the video.

## Cadence (from the distribution plan)

- **Day 1:** the full 90 s everywhere. TikTok Creator Rewards needs videos over 1 minute, and the full film is the one that
  pays, so post it first and pin it on the profile.
- **Days 2–3:** one or two 30 s cuts per platform. Each ends on "FULL 90 SECONDS ON MY PROFILE" and sends people back to the full film.
- **Time:** post in the audience's evening. For US sports, 7–10 pm US Eastern.
- **News beats the queue.** If a subject makes news (Ronaldo's 1,000th goal, opening night), post that episode first.
