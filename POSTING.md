# Posting playbook — 90 SECONDS

Per-film copy lives in `films/<slug>/post.md`. This file is how to post it.

## The goal right now

Fan videos on the video platforms, with fan-first captions. The caption is about the player and the moment;
AI gets one line at most ("Fan-made with AI" in the YouTube description), and the platform's AI label does the rest.
No "how it's made" in the captions.

## Where

| Platform | File | Copy in `post.md` |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | `<slug>-9x16.mp4` + clips | caption + pinned comment |
| YouTube Shorts | `<slug>-9x16.mp4` + clips | title, description, pinned comment |
| Instagram Reels | `<slug>-9x16.mp4` + clips | caption (+ optional tag of the player) |
| YouTube (16:9, optional) | `<slug>-16x9.mp4` | same title and description; set it as the Short's related video |

On Instagram, switch on "Also share to Facebook" to get Facebook Reels for free.
**Later, not now:** X, Bilibili / 抖音 / 小红书 (each `post.md` keeps a Chinese title for then), LinkedIn.

## Every upload

1. **Upload the original MP4 from `out/`.** Never re-upload a file downloaded from another platform:
   Instagram and YouTube down-rank anything carrying another app's watermark.
2. **Turn on the AI label** where the platform offers one: TikTok "AI-generated content", Instagram "AI info",
   YouTube "altered or synthetic content".
3. **Cover frame:** the still listed at the top of the film's `post.md`.
4. **Hashtags:** 3–5 per post: the player, the team or league, the sport, `#90seconds`. More reads as spam.
5. **Pin the comment** from `post.md`. It is the question that gets people replying.
6. **Reply to comments in the first hour.** That is when each platform decides whether to push the video.
7. **Tags:** at most one, the player's own Instagram account. Don't mass-tag, and don't drop links in comments.

## Cadence

- **Day 1:** the full 90 s on TikTok, Shorts and Reels. TikTok Creator Rewards needs videos over 1 minute, and the full film
  is the one that pays, so post it first and pin it on the profile.
- **Days 2–5:** the film's clips (18–25 s), one a day. Each ends on "FULL 90 SECONDS ON MY PROFILE" and sends people back to the full film.
- **Time:** post in the audience's evening. For US sports, 7–10 pm US Eastern.
- **News beats the queue.** If a subject makes news (Messi's farewell on 6 October, NBA opening night on 20 October,
  Ronaldo's 1,000th goal), post that episode first.
