## Insider references used

- **Two-way**: he pitches and hits, so he is the only player who could face himself. That is the whole game.
- **Sho-Time**: his nickname, the game's title.
- **Trout, full count** (WBC 2023): the most famous at-bat of his career, here the WBC inning.
- **The bat flip** after a home run, and **50/50** with the steal that follows.
- **The yo scale**: the WBC inning's melody turns Japanese.

## Guardrails for this film

- No photos, footage or broadcast audio, and no quotes. Players are pixel likenesses drawn in code.
- The game, "SHO-TIME BASEBALL ’26", is invented. Team colours only: no logos, scripts or caps' insignia. The catcher
  is generic, with no number.
- End card: fan-made, not affiliated with Shohei Ohtani, MLB, any club or WBC organiser.
