**Counting trophies:** totals differ by source. Some lists credit Messi with 48 by counting more minor titles. The film
uses 46, the Wikipedia count that EP03 also uses, and 36 for Ronaldo on the same basis (official team titles).

**The score:** each player wins three rounds. The six categories are the ones fans argue about most, and each is
shown with its real number. The film doesn't pick a winner; the end card asks.

## Insider references used

- **SIUUU**: Ronaldo's jump, spin and landing, here his special move (round 1) and his victory pose.
- **La Pulga**: "the flea", Messi's nickname. His dribbling dash is his special move (round 2).
- **The bicycle kick in Turin** (2018): Ronaldo's round-3 move, for the Champions League.
- **The knuckleball free kick**: Ronaldo's round-5 move. **The curling free kick**: Messi's round-6 move.
- **Lifting the World Cup** (2022): Messi's round-4 move.
- **The GOAT debate**: the select screen leaves four "?" slots for whoever fans name next.

## Guardrails for this film

- No photos, footage or broadcast audio, and no quotes. The players are pixel likenesses drawn in code, never traced.
- The game, "GOAT FIGHT ’26", is invented. No real game's name, logo, sprites, font or sounds. National-team colours
  are used; crests, sponsors and the real World Cup and Champions League trophies are not drawn (the cups are generic
  pixel shapes).
- End card: fan-made, not affiliated with either player, any club, federation or game publisher.
