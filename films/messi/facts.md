"En Barcelona, a 14 de diciembre del 2000 y en presencia de los Sres. Minguella y Horacio, Carles Rexach, secretario
técnico del FC Barcelona, se compromete bajo su responsabilidad y a pesar de algunas opiniones en contra a fichar al
jugador Lionel Messi siempre y cuando nos mantengamos en las cantidades acordadas."

## Insider references used

- **The napkin**: the contract written on a restaurant napkin, "despite some opinions against", and what it sold for.
- **Ronaldinho's pass and the piggyback**: the first goal and the ride off the pitch on his back (Ronaldinho drawn with the curls, headband and smile).
- **The shirt at the Bernabéu**: goal 500, 92nd minute, shirt held up to the home crowd.
- **"¿Qué mirás, bobo? Andá pa' allá."**: Qatar 2022, the line Argentina put on T-shirts.
- **The bisht and the three stars**: Lusail, and the third star over the crest.
- **Pointing to the sky**: for his grandmother Celia.
- **Too small**: the growth-hormone story, answered at the end by a tower of 46 trophies next to a 1.70 m man.

## Guardrails for this film

- No photos, footage or broadcast audio. The only real quote is "¿Qué mirás, bobo? Andá pa' allá", which he said on camera.
  The napkin text is quoted from the document itself.
- Kit colours (Newell's-style red and black, Barcelona, Argentina) are used; crests and sponsor logos are not drawn.
  Trophies are generic pixel shapes.
- His father's death in August 2026, which preceded the retirement, is deliberately left out.
- End card: fan-made, not affiliated with Lionel Messi, the AFA or any club.
