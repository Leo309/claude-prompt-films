## Insider references used

- **"The Chosen One"**: the 2002 magazine-cover nickname (8–12 s).
- **The chalk toss**: his pre-game ritual in Cleveland and Miami, as a pixel sprite that explodes into 43,440 (79–80 s).
- **Down 3–1 → The Block**: the 2016 comeback, and the most replayed defensive play of his career (48–60 s).
- **#23 and #9**: the first father-and-son pair in an NBA game (68–72 s).
- **Season 24 in Philly**: the July 2026 signing, played as a bar of Philly soul (86–88 s).

## Guardrails for this film
- No photos or realistic portraits. People are dots, numbers and type, plus two pixel sprites
  (`sprites.js`) drawn from scratch: a portrait from signature traits (headband, beard, #23) and the chalk-toss pose.
  Neither is traced from a photo, and neither carries a brand mark.
- No team logos, crests or brand marks; cities instead of team names.
- No invented quotes.
- End card: fan-made, not affiliated with the NBA or LeBron James.
