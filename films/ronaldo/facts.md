## Insider references used

- **SIUUU**: the jump, spin and landing celebration, and the crowd chant that comes with it. Pixel sprite in three frames plus a synthesized crowd "SIUUU" (no real voice).
- **The free-kick stance**: the wide-legged set-up and deep breath before a knuckleball (the cold open).
- **Mr. Champions League**: 140 goals and five titles.
- **The bicycle kick in Turin**: the Juventus home crowd on its feet, applauding the man who beat them.
- **"Água!"**: two soda bottles slide off the table (generic, unbranded) and water goes up.
- **The wall of 1,000**: one cell per goal, 21 still empty. The film is built to be re-rendered the day No. 1,000 goes in.

## Guardrails for this film

- No photos, footage or broadcast audio; the only real quote is "Água", which he said on camera.
- Club and national colours are used; crests and sponsor logos are not drawn.
- End card: fan-made, not affiliated with Cristiano Ronaldo or any club.
